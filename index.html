About Asfar

Started in Kano in 2020. Built on quality and repeat customers.

Asfar Fashion began in April 2020 with a collection of handcrafted bags. Since then we have grown into a general bag manufacturer, making backpacks, school bags, laptop bags, travel sets, crossbody bags, totes, lunch bags, pouches and custom-branded orders.

Our customers keep coming back because our bags last and we deliver what we promise. We say thank you with offers like Customer Week, when every order gets 10% off.
